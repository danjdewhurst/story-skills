# Raymond Sallis

## Appearance

Wet from the shoulders up when it is not raining. Smiles the way people smile at cash machines.

## Personality & Traits

Patient, polite, and certain he will get what he came for.

## Backstory

Signed for four weekly parcels at Harrowgate Street in April 2013. Calls Fola his wife; nobody has confirmed it.

## Motivations & Goals

To get the suitcase, or the ledger, or the page that was cut from it.

## Voice & Speech Patterns

Statements, not questions: "You'll be here tomorrow night."
