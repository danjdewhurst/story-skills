# Chapter 4: The Storm

## Chapter Text

You come up into the lamp room with the oil can hugged to your chest. You fill the tank, trim the wick, and put a match to it. The lens takes the flame and throws it out across the water.

Out past the reef, a ship's lights rise and fall. She is standing in toward the rocks, too close to turn by the light alone. Somewhere below you, Tobias Wren is waiting on a broken ankle, and he cannot climb.

Below the gallery hangs the storm bell. Its rope runs down the outside of the tower. You cannot tend the wick and ring the bell. You have to choose.
