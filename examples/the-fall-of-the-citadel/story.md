# The Fall of the Citadel

## Synopsis

Twelve years before The Last Ember, General Maren warns King Aldric that the border wars will break the Northern Reach. When the king refuses to turn the embers into weapons, Maren takes the Ashen Citadel by force, and sixteen-year-old Sera Voss has one night to get her brother out alive.

## Tone & Style

A prequel written with the ending already known: every warm scene in the citadel is shadowed by what readers of The Last Ember know is coming. Closer and more claustrophobic than the first book, with the citadel still glowing and the magic still strong. Maren is a point-of-view presence here, and his reasoning should be persuasive enough to hurt.

## Series Notes

- Book 2 in publication order, but set first in the chronology of the Ember Cycle.
- Canon fixed by The Last Ember: King Aldric and Queen Elara die in the coup; Sera (16) and Kael (12) escape through the tunnels into the Whispering Vale; Maren seals the ember well and burns out his own affinity.
- Sera, Kael, and Maren must end this book alive and in the states The Last Ember opens with.
