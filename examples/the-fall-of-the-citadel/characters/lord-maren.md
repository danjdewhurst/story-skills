# Lord Maren

## Appearance

Iron-grey only at the temples, both hands whole. He still wears the ember-red of the king's guard, and the ember-stone on his gauntlet still glows when he grips it.

## Personality & Traits

Loyal, blunt, and running out of patience. He has buried too many soldiers on the border to accept another year of the king's restraint. He loves the Voss children in a gruff, distant way, which makes what he does worse.

**Strengths:** Strategic brilliance, soldiers who would die for him, a working ember affinity
**Flaws:** Believes that being right entitles him to act

## Motivations & Goals

**Primary:** Arm the Northern Reach before the border breaks
**Internal:** Prove that the Voss idealism is killing the people it claims to protect
**Fear:** Watching the realm fall while he obeys orders

## Voice & Speech Patterns

Measured even here, but warmer than he will be twelve years later. His famous silences have not hardened yet.

> "I am not asking you to stop being kind, Majesty. I am asking you to stop being slow."

## Character Arc

**Starting state:** The king's most trusted general
**End state:** Usurper, with his own affinity burned out, the canon starting point for The Last Ember

## Series Canon

Must end this book alive, holding the citadel, with the ember well sealed and his left hand withered.
