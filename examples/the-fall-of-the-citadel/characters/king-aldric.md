# King Aldric Voss

## Appearance

Broad, soft-spoken, with ember-light that pools in his palms whenever he is thinking. His hair went grey early from pouring his own power into the citadel's walls.

## Personality & Traits

Patient to a fault. Believes the embers belong to the land, not the army, and that a realm defended by weapons it fears will not stay free.

**Strengths:** Deep affinity, the love of the Lower Warrens
**Flaws:** Mistakes delay for wisdom

## Voice & Speech Patterns

Slow, warm, and fond of questions he already knows the answers to.

> "And when the border is safe, General, who will you point the embers at next?"

## Series Canon

Dies in the coup together with Queen Elara. The Last Ember treats both deaths as backstory, so on the page he dies in this book. When that chapter is drafted, set `status: deceased` and `died-in` here.
