## Chapter Text

Petra came every Thursday, and this Thursday she brought the harbour-master's boy. Ewan, twelve, all elbows. He wanted to see the light.

I took him up. He put his hand flat on the brass of the lens frame before I could stop him, and I showed him where the oldest prism has a crack you can only see at noon, a hair of light running the wrong way.

He found the chest himself. Children do. He ran his thumb over the side of it and said, "There's another keyhole here, Mr Reyes. A little one." I had never seen it. Thirty-one years in that room and I had never seen it.

I told him it was nothing and took him down to the boat.
