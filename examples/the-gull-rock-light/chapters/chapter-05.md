# Chapter 5: The Bell

## Outline

1. **The bell** - Ada rings the storm bell until her hands bleed
2. **The turn** - The ship hears it and turns off the reef
3. **Ending** - Dawn, the lamp guttered out but the ship safe

---

## Chapter Text

You go out onto the gallery and haul on the bell rope. The note goes out flat and huge across the water. You ring until your palms tear, and then you wrap them in your scarf and ring again.

The ship's lights swing. Slowly, as if it has all night, it turns its shoulder to the reef and stands off into deep water.

At dawn the lamp is out again, its wick burned down to nothing, and Tobias is laughing on the watch-room floor. "First night," he says. "You'll do." THE END.
