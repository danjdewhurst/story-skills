The lamp room is all glass and brass and the smell of paraffin. The Fresnel stands in the middle of it like a beehive made of prisms, and when I climb the ladder the light catches every edge at once.

Dusk comes through the glass in panels. I trim the wicks, check the oil, and wipe yesterday's soot off the prisms with a rag. The brass key is still in my pocket. It can stay there until I decide what it means.
