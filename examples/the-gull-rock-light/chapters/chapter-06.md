---

## Chapter Text

You stay with the lamp. You trim the wick, polish the glass, and keep the flame high and white while the wind tries every window.

Near midnight you hear it: a long tearing groan from the outer reef. The ship has struck.

At dawn the lifeboat from the mainland takes her crew off one by one, all of them alive, and the ship breaks up on the next tide. Tobias watches from the gallery with his arm across your shoulders. "You kept the light," he says. It does not feel like enough. THE END.
