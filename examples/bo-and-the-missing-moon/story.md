# Bo and the Missing Moon

## Synopsis

On a foggy night, a young badger finds the moon gone and decides someone has taken it. He asks Wick the owl and Old Pike in the pond, and neither has it. Sitting alone in the fog, Bo sees a silver glow on top of Lantern Hill, climbs above the fog, and finds the moon where it always was. He runs home to tell Nana Bracken how to find it.

This example is a 32-page picture book: each chapter is one spread (`chapter-01` is spread 1, pages 4-5), with one scene record per spread for who and where the picture shows, and a chapter `hook` for what each page turn does. The art plan and spread briefs are in `adaptations/picture-book/pagination.md`, not in the chapter text.

## Tone & Style

Read-aloud bedtime text in short lines. Refrains carry the structure: "Not anywhere" as Bo searches, and Nana's "Back to bed", which changes once at the end. The words never describe what the picture shows; spread 12 is three words long.

## Notes

All 14 spreads are drafted, so the book is at `revising`. Rule of three: Wick, Old Pike, then Lantern Hill. Bo, not an adult, solves the problem.

`story pacing` warns that the spreads have no sequel units, that the last spreads all end in a plain yes, and that spread 12 is short. A picture book runs on page turns rather than scene and sequel, and spread 12 is the reveal the picture carries, so those warnings stay as they are.
