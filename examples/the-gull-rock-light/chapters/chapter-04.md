# Chapter 4: The Storm

## Outline

---

## Chapter Text

You fill the tank and put a match to the wick. The lens takes the flame and throws it out across the water, and for a moment you think it is enough.

It is not. The ship is still standing in toward the reef, too close to turn by the light alone. Below the gallery hangs the storm bell, and the bell rope runs down the outside of the tower.

You cannot tend the wick and ring the bell. You have to choose.
