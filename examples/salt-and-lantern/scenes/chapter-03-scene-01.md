# The Viewing

## Purpose

During a viewing of the decommissioned lighthouse, the last keeper's daughter lights the old lamp one more time.

## Continuity Notes

The lamp is lit with the paraffin and matches from the cupboard under the gallery door. The automated beacon is on the pier.
