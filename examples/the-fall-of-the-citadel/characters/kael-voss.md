# Kael Voss

## Appearance

Small for twelve, all elbows and scraped knees. Dark Voss hair, no amber in his eyes. Always has a pocket full of chalk from mapping the tunnels under the Middle Ring.

## Personality & Traits

Curious, watchful, and quietly ashamed that he has no ember affinity. He copes by knowing things nobody else does: which guards sleep on watch, which doors stick, where the old passages lead.

**Strengths:** Memory for routes, patience, notices what adults ignore
**Flaws:** Hides when frightened, thinks he is the family's spare

## Voice & Speech Patterns

Asks a lot of questions. Answers his own questions when nobody else will.

> "The Whisper Gate isn't a story. I've touched it."

## Character Arc

**Starting state:** The overlooked second child who plays in tunnels
**End state:** His tunnel maps get them both out alive

## Series Canon

Must end this book alive and escape with Sera. He remembers the escape more than he remembers his parents.
