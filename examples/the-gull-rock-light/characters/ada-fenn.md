# Ada Fenn

## Appearance

Twenty-four, broad-shouldered from rowing, hands already scarred by rope.

## Personality & Traits

Practical and stubborn. She does the next job in front of her and worries later.

## Backstory

The daughter of a harbor pilot, sent out as relief keeper on her first posting.

## Motivations & Goals

She wants to prove she can hold a station alone; she needs to learn that keeping a light means choosing what to lose.

## Voice & Speech Patterns

The reader is Ada: the story speaks to her as "you", so her voice is in what she notices rather than what she says.

## Character Arc

- **Starting state:** A relief keeper who has never stood a night watch alone
- **Key turning points:** The dark lamp; finding Tobias or his coat; the bell or the wick
- **Ending state:** A keeper who has made a keeper's choice and lived with it
