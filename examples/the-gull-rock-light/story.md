# The Gull Rock Light

## Synopsis

You are Ada Fenn, the relief keeper, landing on Gull Rock on the night its lamp goes dark. You choose whether to search the rocks or climb the tower, and when a ship stands in toward the reef, whether to ring the storm bell or keep the light.

This example is a branching story: chapters carry `choices`, and `story build --format twee` turns them into a Twine story.

## Tone & Style

Second person, present tense, short passages. Every choice is a keeper's choice between two duties, and both endings cost something.
