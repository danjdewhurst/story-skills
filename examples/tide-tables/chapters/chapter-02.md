# Chapter 2: Slack Water

## Outline

1. **Stillness** - the tide turns and nothing moves
2. **Boats** - swing round and face nowhere
3. **Table** - the minute the tide table leaves out

---

## Chapter Text

For a quarter of an hour
nothing comes in and nothing goes.

The boats swing round on their ropes
and face no way in particular.

I hold the tide table up to the light,
the small grey numbers of the day,
and find this minute is not on it:
the pause between two columns,
the breath the printer left.
