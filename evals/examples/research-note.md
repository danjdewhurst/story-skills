# Paraffin vapour burner

## Question

How did a keeper light and tend a paraffin vapour burner in a first-order light in 1958: was it warmed with methylated spirit before the paraffin was let through, how often was the tank pumped through the night, and did a mantle need changing mid-watch?

## Search Plan

- Primary: lighthouse service instructions to keepers for paraffin vapour burners from the 1950s, in national archives or the lighthouse authority's own records.
- Primary: keepers' memoirs, oral-history recordings, and station logbooks from Scottish rock lights of the period.
- Primary: a museum that holds a working burner, and its curator, who could show the lighting sequence.
- Secondary: histories of lighthouse illumination and the burner makers' trade literature.
- People: a retired keeper or a keeper's family member, by interview, with consent.
- Enough: one keepers' instruction manual from the period, or two independent first-hand accounts that agree.

## Findings

None yet. Chapter 2's three details (methylated spirit to warm the burner, pumping every few hours, a mantle changed at midnight) are recalled, not sourced, and stay unverified until a source above confirms or corrects them.

## Story Use

Chapter 2 uses all three details. Revise it if the findings contradict them.
