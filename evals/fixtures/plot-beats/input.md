Story: "The Key on the Door" — quiet coastal mystery, novella. Structure: three-act (plot/_index.md).
POV: first person, past tense. Narrator: Tomas Reyes, keeper of Greywidow Light.

Established facts:
- The lamp is a first-order Fresnel lens, paraffin-fired. Tomas refused the Board's electric conversion twice.
- Tomas's wife Ana died four winters ago. Her sea-chest is in the lamp room; Tomas has never opened it.
- Petra Lindqvist skippers the supply boat; she calls every second Thursday.

Arc: plot/arcs/the-key.md (type: main, characters: tomas-reyes, petra-lindqvist)

Chapters in the book (these are all of them; the book has eight):
- chapter-01 "The Key on the Door": at dusk Tomas finds a brass key on the lamp-room door.
- chapter-02 "Thursday": Petra's boat calls; Tomas says nothing about the key.
- chapter-03 "The Lock": the key snaps in the sea-chest's lock.
- chapter-04 "What Ana Kept": fixed midpoint. The chest is opened ashore; Ana's logbook is inside.
- chapter-05 "The Board's Letter": the Board orders the electric conversion.
- chapter-06 "Fog": the lamp fails in fog; Tomas keeps it lit by hand through the night.
- chapter-07 "The Last Entry": Tomas reads the logbook's last entry and confronts Petra.
- chapter-08 "Paraffin": Tomas chooses what to keep.

Author's note: the midpoint is fixed at chapter 4. Do not move it.
