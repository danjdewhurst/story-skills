# Bo

## Appearance

A young badger, small enough to sit in Nana's lap, with a stripe that has not finished growing. His nose is always the first part of him anywhere.

## Personality & Traits

Certain, then curious. When something is wrong, Bo decides who did it first and asks questions after.

## Backstory

Bo lives with Nana Bracken in the sett at the bottom of the Hollow. Saying night-night to the moon is the last thing he does every day.

## Motivations & Goals

He wants the moon back where it belongs. He needs to learn that a thing he cannot see may not be gone.

## Voice & Speech Patterns

Short, direct questions, said at full volume:

- "Wick! Did you take the moon?"
- "Then who did?"
- "Up."

## Character Arc

- **Starting state:** Sure that someone has taken the moon
- **Key turning points:** Wick and Old Pike have not seen it (spreads 4-7); the glow on Lantern Hill (spread 9)
- **Ending state:** Knows the moon was above the fog all along, and tells Nana how to find it
