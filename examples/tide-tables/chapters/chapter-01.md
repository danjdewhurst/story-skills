# Chapter 1: Dawn Ebb

## Outline

1. **Ebb** - the estuary empties before dawn
2. **Walk** - out along the channel as it runs
3. **Heron** - an image to close on

---

## Chapter Text

The estuary lets go before I wake.
By first light it has given back
the posts, the chains, the green-black stones
it borrowed in the night.

I walk the hard sand out
to where the channel still remembers
which way it ran,
and it is running out.

A heron lifts, unhurried,
as if the morning were a coat
it has worn before.
