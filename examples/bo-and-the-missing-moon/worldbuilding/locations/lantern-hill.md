# Lantern Hill

## Description

The hill above the Hollow, brambles and roots on the way up and short grass at the top.

## History

Named for the way its top catches the light first at dawn and last at dusk.

## Culture & Customs

None: nobody in the Hollow climbs it at night.

## Notable Features

- The bramble slope, where Bo collects his burrs
- The bare top, which stands above the fog

## Current State

Its top is clear of the fog, under a full moon.
