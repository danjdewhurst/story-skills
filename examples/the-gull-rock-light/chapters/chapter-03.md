# Chapter 3: The Tower

## Outline

1. **The stair** - Ada climbs the tower in the dark
2. **Tobias** - She finds Tobias on the watch-room floor with a broken ankle
3. **Reveal** - He tried to fetch oil from the store and fell

---

## Chapter Text

The stair turns and turns. Halfway up, you hear breathing that is not your own.

Tobias Wren is on the watch-room floor with his ankle bent the wrong way. "Oil store," he says through his teeth. "Tank ran dry. I went down for more and the stair came up to meet me."

You get him against the wall with his coat under his head. The oil can is beside him, full. He did the hard part. You pick it up and climb on.
