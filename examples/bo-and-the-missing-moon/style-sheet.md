# Style Sheet

The book's house decisions, kept the way a copyeditor keeps them. Read this before drafting or revising prose. `story prose` enforces the lists in the frontmatter: `dialect` (british, american, or unspecified) flags the other dialect's common spellings, each `preferred` entry flags its `avoid` form, `watch-words` are counted in every chapter, and `allow-words` silences a built-in filter word or adverb. Add a `samples` list of your own prose (`../book-one`, approved chapters) and `story prose` compares each chapter with it instead of fixed limits.

## Voice

Read-aloud text for a parent and a child of three to six. Short lines, one picture moment per spread, and no description of what the art shows.

> Not over the oak. Not over the stream. Not anywhere.

> There it was.

## Spelling And Usage

British spelling. Bo's word for the ritual is "night-night", never "goodnight".

## Capitalisation

The Hollow, Lantern Hill, Old Pike, Nana Bracken. "Moon" is capitalised only when Bo speaks to it.

## Hyphenation And Compounds

night-night

## Numbers, Dates, And Time

None in the text.

## Dialogue And Punctuation

Double quotes. "Said" only. Ellipses only for the page turns in spreads 1 and 2.

## Character Voices

- **Bo:** Questions and one-word decisions ("Up.").
- **Nana Bracken:** "Back to bed."
- **Wick:** "Who, me?"
- **Old Pike:** Slow, long sentences, the longest in the book.

## Watch List

`very` belongs to spread 6, where it slows the rise of Old Pike. Keep it out of every other spread.
