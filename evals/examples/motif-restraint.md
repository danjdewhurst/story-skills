Dusk came up the stairs ahead of me. I filled the reservoir, trimmed the wick to a hand's breadth, and struck the match on the door frame, one pass, no fuss.

The paraffin caught. The Fresnel took the flame and gave it back multiplied, out over the water to where the Skerry boats would be watching for it.

The brass key stayed in my pocket through all of it. I wiped the soot off the prism I had missed yesterday, logged the hour and the wind, and went down with the light burning behind me.

Four winters. The lamp was lit. That was the whole of the night's work.
