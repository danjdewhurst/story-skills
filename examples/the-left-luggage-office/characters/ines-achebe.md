# Ines Achebe

## Appearance

Twenty-four. Station fleece, reading glasses pushed up into her hair, a flask of her mother's tea.

## Personality & Traits

Quietly observant. She makes a pencil tick on the back of every ticket she issues, because nobody else would think to.

## Backstory

Her father worked at Harrowgate Street for twenty-two years, latterly in the parcels office, and retired soon after the year Aunty Fola left. Ines took the night job because it was close to home and nothing happened there.

## Motivations & Goals

She wants a quiet life and her mother's approval. She needs to know what her family stopped talking about when she was eleven.

## Voice & Speech Patterns

Precise and polite, especially when refusing:

- "It is the right number. It isn't the right ticket."
- "I know. That's why."

## Character Arc

- **Starting state:** Keeps out of things
- **Key turning points:** Refuses Sallis (episode 1); the birthday card (episode 2); the key (episode 3)
- **Ending state:** Season one ends with her holding the ledger Sallis wants
