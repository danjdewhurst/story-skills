# Left Luggage Office

## Description

A counter and a cage of numbered shelves under the arches, off the main concourse. A bell on the counter, a lamp, a collections book.

## History

Open since the station was rebuilt. Run by two clerks: Pat Dunmore on days, Ines Achebe on nights.

## Culture & Customs

Tickets are torn in two: the customer keeps the top half, the counterfoil goes on the bag. A collection is signed for in the book.

## Notable Features

- Shelf nine, at the back, where the blue suitcase went
- The clock at the top of the arches, where Ines catches Dunmore

## Current State

Shelf nine is empty.
