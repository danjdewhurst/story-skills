I took the brass key from my pocket and weighed it in my palm. It wanted the sea-chest; there was no other lock on the rock it could fit, and I had carried it three days without trying.

Not yet. A thing shut for four winters can stay shut one night more. I put the key back and trimmed the lamp instead, and wrote the day in the board slate: boat called, oil full, wind NE.

Whoever left the key on the lamp-room door knows I found it. Let them wait.
