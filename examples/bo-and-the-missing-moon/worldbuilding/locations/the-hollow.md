# The Hollow

## Description

A bowl of woodland at the foot of Lantern Hill: a badger sett under an oak, a stream, an ash tree, and a black pond at the bottom.

## History

Fog collects here on still nights and stays until morning.

## Culture & Customs

Nana and Bo say night-night to the moon from the sett door.

## Notable Features

- The sett door under the oak, where the book starts and ends
- Wick's ash tree on the path down to the pond
- The pond, where Old Pike lives

## Current State

Full of fog, up to just below the top of Lantern Hill.
