INT. LAMP ROOM - DUSK

[[Source: chapter-02-scene-01]]

Glass on every side, going grey. A first-order Fresnel lens stands cold in the middle of the floor.

TOMAS REYES, 58, comes up the last of the stairs in a keeper's jersey. The shape of a brass key shows through the cloth of his trouser pocket.

He fills the paraffin reservoir. Trims the wick. Strikes a match. It gutters out. He strikes another and the flame takes.

The light climbs the prisms and goes out across the water.

Under a shelf of spare mantles: a sea-chest, salt-stained, shut. Tomas turns his head towards it, then back to the lamp.

He starts down the stairs. Two steps down he stops, comes back up, and locks the lamp-room door behind him.
