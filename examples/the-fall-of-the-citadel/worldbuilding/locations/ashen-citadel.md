# The Ashen Citadel

## Description

The same fortress-city as in The Last Ember, but twelve years younger and still warm. The ember-stone veins in the basalt walls glow steadily, frost never reaches the Middle Ring, and the Festival of Embers is still celebrated openly.

## Notable Features

- **The Ember Well** — open, tended by the king himself
- **The Whisper Gate** — known to the Voss family, and to one twelve-year-old who has been mapping the tunnels
- **The Border Barracks** — newly overcrowded with Maren's returning wounded

## Series Canon

By the end of this book, the ember well is sealed and Maren holds the High Keep. The Last Ember opens with the city in decline.
