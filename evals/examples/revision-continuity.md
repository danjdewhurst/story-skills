# Chapter 3: Three Days

## Chapter Text

I took the brass key from my pocket and weighed it in my palm. Three days I had carried it, and never once tried it in the lock. It wanted the sea-chest; there was no other lock on the rock it could fit.

I knelt by the chest and kept my hands on my knees. A thing shut four winters can stay shut one night more. Whoever left the key on the lamp-room door knows I found it. Let them wait.
