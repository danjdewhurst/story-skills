I climbed to the lamp room at dusk with the brass key in my pocket. The Fresnel stood cold. I filled the reservoir, trimmed the wick, and struck a match. The paraffin caught on the second try. Below the spare mantles, Ana's sea-chest sat where it had sat for four winters. I did not look at it for long. I locked the lamp-room door behind me when I went down, which I had never done before.
