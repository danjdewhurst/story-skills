# Nana Bracken

## Appearance

An old badger in a knitted shawl, grey all the way to the tip of her nose.

## Personality & Traits

Unhurried and sleepy. She has seen a great many foggy nights.

## Backstory

She has said night-night to the moon from the same door for longer than anyone can remember.

## Motivations & Goals

She wants Bo in bed. She does not solve the mystery for him.

## Voice & Speech Patterns

Three words, said the same way each time, until the last spread:

- "Back to bed."

## Character Arc

- **Starting state:** Yawns at Bo's theory
- **Key turning points:** None on the page; she is the fixed point Bo comes home to
- **Ending state:** Smiles at what Bo has found
