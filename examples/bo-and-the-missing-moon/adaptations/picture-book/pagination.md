## Spread Briefs

### Spread 1 (pages 4-5)

- **Text:** chapter-01
- **Moment:** Bo and Nana look up from the sett door, as they do every night
- **Characters:** Bo (excited), Nana Bracken (sleepy, in her shawl)
- **Setting:** The sett door under the oak, the-hollow; clear night, full moon
- **Must show:** The oak, the stream, and the moon above them, so spread 2 can show the same view without it
- **Must not show:** Any fog
- **Text placement:** Left page, low; the moon top right
- **Continuity:** Nana's shawl is the same in spreads 1, 3, and 14

### Spread 2 (pages 6-7)

- **Text:** chapter-02
- **Moment:** The same view as spread 1, filled with fog
- **Characters:** Bo, alone in the doorway
- **Setting:** the-hollow, fog up to the treetops
- **Must show:** The outline of the oak, and where the moon was
- **Must not show:** The top of Lantern Hill
- **Text placement:** Across the fog, top
- **Continuity:** The fog line stays at the same height until spread 11

### Spread 9 (pages 20-21)

- **Text:** chapter-09
- **Moment:** Bo looks up and sees the silver glow
- **Characters:** Bo, from behind, looking up
- **Setting:** the-hollow, Lantern Hill rising above the fog
- **Must show:** The glow, faint; it appears for the first time here
- **Must not show:** The moon itself
- **Text placement:** Bottom left, in the fog
- **Continuity:** The glow is absent from spreads 1-8

### Spread 12 (pages 26-27)

- **Text:** chapter-12
- **Moment:** The moon, round and enormous, resting on top of the fog
- **Characters:** Bo, small, at the bottom edge
- **Setting:** The top of lantern-hill
- **Must show:** The fog below, like a white sea, with the Hollow hidden under it
- **Must not show:** Anyone else
- **Text placement:** Three words, bottom right
- **Art note:** The moon is not on the hill. It is far away in the sky, just above the fog.

### Page 32

- **Text:** none
- **Moment:** Bo and Nana at the top of Lantern Hill, both saying night-night to the moon
- **Continuity:** Nana's shawl; Bo's burrs
