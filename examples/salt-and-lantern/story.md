# Salt and Lantern

## Synopsis

Three short stories by three writers, gathered around one stretch of coast: a mudflat at low water, a salt works in its last season, and a lighthouse being emptied for sale. Each story keeps its own characters; the book shares only the sea.

This example is an anthology. `editor` in this file credits Miriam Hale on the title pages, and each chapter is one contributor's story with its own `author`, which the builds print under the story's title and keep out of the word count.

## Tone & Style

Quiet, close third person. Each story is under five hundred words and ends on a held breath rather than a resolution.

## Notes

The stories were written and submitted as separate projects; this project only assembles them. See docs/series.md, Short-story collections and anthologies.
