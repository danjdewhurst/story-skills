# Folake Achebe

## Appearance

Fifties, a green coat buttoned to the neck. Counts out coins as if each one costs her something.

## Personality & Traits

Careful to the point of secrecy. Trusts handwriting more than people.

## Backstory

Grace's younger sister. Left thirteen years ago after a row with Ines's father about the station. The family said she went to Lagos.

## Motivations & Goals

To keep the suitcase, and the ledger, away from Raymond Sallis without putting Ines in danger. She has not yet managed both.

## Voice & Speech Patterns

Short instructions: "One week. And don't open it. Whatever it says."

## Character Arc

- **Starting state:** In hiding
- **Key turning points:** Leaves the case with Ines; collects it in Ines's name
- **Ending state:** Off the page in season one, always one step ahead
