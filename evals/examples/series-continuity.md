## Series Notes

- Sequel to The Key on the Door (`../the-key-on-the-door`), set the spring after it ends.
- Tomas Reyes held Greywidow Light 31 years. Ana died four winters before book one, when a storm kept the supply boat from the rock; she stays dead and goes in `mentions`, never a cast.
- The lamp stays a first-order Fresnel lens, paraffin-fired. Tomas refused the Board's electric conversion twice.
- Petra Lindqvist still skippers the supply boat and calls every second Thursday.
- Ana's sea-chest stays shut in the lamp room, and Tomas keeps the brass key.
- Open thread: who left the brass key on the lamp-room door. Book two leaves it open.
