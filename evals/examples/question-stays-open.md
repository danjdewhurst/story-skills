I checked the door the way you check a thing you already know. No mark on the paint, no scratch at the lock. The brass key sat in my palm, dark at the edges, warm from my pocket.

Nobody climbs to the lamp room but me. The Board sends letters, not men.

So who? The wind doesn't carry keys. The gulls don't, whatever they claim.

I locked the lamp room behind me and put the key back in my pocket. Whoever wanted me to have it knows where I keep my evenings — so who climbed the rock to leave it?
