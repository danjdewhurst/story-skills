Dusk. I trimmed the wick and filled the lamp with paraffin, and it took the flame the way it always has.

Then I knelt at Ana's sea-chest. Four winters under the spare mantles. I had the brass key out of my pocket before I knew I meant to, and I held it over the lock and did not try it.

The lamp turned above me. The rock went dark.

I put the key back in my pocket. It had been hanging on the lamp-room door, and there is one stair up to this room, and I have climbed it alone for four winters.

So who else had climbed them?
