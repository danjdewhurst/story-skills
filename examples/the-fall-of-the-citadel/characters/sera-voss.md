# Sera Voss

## Appearance

Sixteen and already taller than her mother. No grey in her hair yet and no scar on her jaw; both arrive by the end of this book. Her eyes glow amber only when she is angry, and she has not learned to hide it.

## Personality & Traits

Proud, impatient, certain that the citadel will always be there. She treats ember lessons as a birthright rather than a discipline and argues with her mother about everything. Fierce with Kael, whom she teases and protects in equal measure.

**Strengths:** Raw ember affinity, courage, knows every passage in the High Keep
**Flaws:** Arrogance, assumes adults have things under control

## Motivations & Goals

**Primary:** Be taken seriously as heir
**Internal:** Wants her father's approval more than she will admit
**Fear:** Being ordinary

## Voice & Speech Patterns

Quick, sharp, more words than she will use at twenty-eight. Her dry humour is already there, but it is playful rather than cutting.

> "If General Maren frowns any harder, the walls will crack."

## Character Arc

**Starting state:** A sheltered heir who believes the citadel is permanent
**End state:** Carries Kael out through the tunnels, the canon starting point for The Last Ember

## Series Canon

Must end this book alive, scarred from jaw to collarbone, and in the Whispering Vale with Kael.
