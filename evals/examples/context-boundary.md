Dusk. I trimmed the wick and filled the lamp with paraffin, and it took the flame the way it has for thirty-one years.

Then I knelt at Ana's sea-chest. I had the brass key out of my pocket before I knew I meant to, and I held it over the lock and did not try it.

The lamp turned above me. The rock went dark.

I put the key back in my pocket. It had been hanging on the lamp-room door at dusk, and nobody comes up to this room but me.

So who had climbed my stairs?
