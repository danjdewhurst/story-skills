# Old Pike

## Appearance

A huge, slow pike. Only an eye and whiskers ever break the water.

## Personality & Traits

Ancient, solemn, and a little sad.

## Backstory

## Motivations & Goals

To be left in peace at the bottom of the pond.

## Voice & Speech Patterns
