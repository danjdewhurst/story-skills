# Chapter 2: The Rocks

---

## Chapter Text

You follow the rope down to the east ledge. Spray soaks you to the knees. Wedged between two stones you find an oilskin coat with the name WREN stitched inside the collar.

There is no one inside it.

Out past the reef, a ship's lights rise and fall. It is closer than it should be, and without the lamp it has nothing to steer by. You leave the coat where it is and run for the tower. Tobias is on the stair, his ankle broken, a full oil can beside him. "Tank ran dry," he says. "Go."
