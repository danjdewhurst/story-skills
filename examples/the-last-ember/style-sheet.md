# Style Sheet

The book's house decisions, kept the way a copyeditor keeps them. Read this before drafting or revising prose. `story prose` enforces the lists in the frontmatter: `dialect` (british, american, or unspecified) flags the other dialect's common spellings, each `preferred` entry flags its `avoid` form, `watch-words` are counted in every chapter, and `allow-words` silences a built-in filter word or adverb.

## Voice

Close third person, past tense, locked to the POV character. Short declarative sentences carry tension; longer sentences are saved for magic and memory. The prose is spare and physical: heat, bark, moss, stone. It never explains a feeling the body has already shown.

> The grove was quieter than it should have been.

## Spelling And Usage

British spelling (grey, humour, defences) with one house exception: *toward*, not *towards*. Compound magic terms are hyphenated: *ember-stone*, *ember-burn*.

## Capitalisation

The Heart Grove, the Whispering Vale, the Ashen Citadel, the Lost Heir, the Northern Reach. *Lord Maren* on first reference in a scene, *Maren* after. Embers and ember motes stay lower case.

## Hyphenation And Compounds

ember-stone, ember-burn, root-knotted, mud-spattered. *Pre-dawn* keeps its hyphen.

## Numbers, Dates, And Time

Spell out numbers under one hundred in prose: twelve years, seven embers. Time is told by light (dawn, pre-dawn, dusk), not clocks.

## Dialogue And Punctuation

Double quotation marks. Closed em dashes, used for interruptions and for asides in narration. *Said* and *asked* only; show manner through action beats. Italics for stressed words, never for thought.

## Character Voices

One-line summaries for drafting; each character file's Voice & Speech Patterns section is canon.

- **[Sera Voss](characters/sera-voss.md)** — direct and economical; clipped when stressed; anger goes quiet, not loud.
- **[Kael Voss](characters/kael-voss.md)** — warmer, conversational; humour to deflect; asks rather than demands; voice tightens when afraid.
- **[Lord Maren](characters/lord-maren.md)** — precise and measured; says no more than necessary; silence as a weapon; bleak dry wit.

## Watch List

- *almost* — Sera's hesitations lean on it; one per scene at most.
- *something* — replace with the concrete thing whenever the POV character could name it.
- *quietly* is allowed: Kael's lowered voice is a deliberate beat.
