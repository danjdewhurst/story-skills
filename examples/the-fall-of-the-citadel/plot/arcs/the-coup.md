# The Coup

## Setup

The border wars are going badly. General Maren returns from the front and asks King Aldric to turn the ember well into a weapon. The king refuses in front of the court, including his daughter.

## Rising Action

Maren quietly moves loyal companies into the Middle Ring. Sera notices the new faces on the walls but mistakes them for a parade guard. Kael finds soldiers using a tunnel that should be forgotten.

## Climax

On the night of the Festival of Embers, Maren takes the High Keep. The king and queen die defending the ember well. Sera takes the scar on her jaw holding a door.

## Resolution

Kael's tunnel maps lead the siblings through the Whisper Gate into the Whispering Vale. Maren seals the well and burns out his own affinity to prove he will never rule by it. This hands off to the opening of The Last Ember.
