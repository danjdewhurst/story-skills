# Style Sheet

## Voice

Close first person, past tense: Tomas Reyes, dry and spare. Short sentences and fragments ("Dusk, and the lamp lit."), feeling shown through what he does, never named. Plain register; the only flourish is a hard, practical simile. Sounds right:

> I watched them the way you watch a clock you don't trust.
>
> Hundred and fourteen steps; I don't count them, my knees do.

## Spelling And Usage

British spelling (*harbour*, *grey*), as `language: en-GB` and the draft agree.

Open question for the author: chapter 1 has "Okay" and chapter 2 has "OK". Which form is house style? I have not added a `preferred` entry until you choose.

## Capitalisation

*the sound* lower case; *the Board* capitalised.

## Hyphenation And Compounds

*lamp room* as two words.

## Numbers, Dates, And Time

Numbers in words (*Hundred and fourteen steps*).

## Dialogue And Punctuation

Double quotes, plain *said*, action beats.
