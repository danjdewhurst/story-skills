Greywidow carries into book two unchanged: Tomas Reyes held the light 31 years, and Ana died four winters ago when a storm kept the supply boat from the rock.

The lamp stays a first-order Fresnel lens, paraffin-fired — the Board's conversion refused twice. Petra Lindqvist still skippers the supply boat every second Thursday.

Ana's sea-chest stays shut in the lamp room. The brass key stays untried, and who left it on the door stays open.
