# Chapter 1: The Landing

## Outline

1. **Arrival** - The supply boat leaves Ada on Gull Rock at dusk
2. **The dark lamp** - The light is out and keeper Tobias Wren does not answer
3. **Choice** - Search the rocks or climb the tower

---

## Chapter Text

The supply boat backs off the landing before your boots are dry. The boatman does not wait for thanks. He has seen what you have seen: the lamp at the top of Gull Rock is dark, and the sun is already down.

You call for Tobias Wren. The keeper you have come to relieve should be on the landing with a lantern and a list of complaints. The only answer is the sea working at the rocks.

The tower door stands open. A coil of rope lies across the path to the east ledge, where the rock drops to the water.
