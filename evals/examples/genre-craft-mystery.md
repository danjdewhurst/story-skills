The contract holds: one brass key, found on the lamp-room door at dusk, and one shut sea-chest it must fit. Petra's boat keeps its Thursdays; the lamp keeps its paraffin. Every clue is on the page and none of them names a hand.

I turn the key over and say nothing. The rock keeps its weather, the Board keeps its letters, and the door keeps its silence — but whose convenience did that silence serve?
