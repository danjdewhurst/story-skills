Story: "The Key on the Door" — quiet coastal mystery.
Petra Lindqvist, 40s, skippers the supply boat out of Skerry. Loud, cheerful, fond of a joke at Tomas's expense. She shouts comic rhymes up to the rock as she comes alongside; Tomas never answers them.

Established facts:
- Greywidow Light stands on a rock off the village of Skerry.
- Tomas Reyes, 58, has kept the light for 31 years. The lamp is paraffin-fired; he refused the Board's electric conversion twice.
- Petra calls every second Thursday with paraffin and stores.

Continuity:
- Open question: who left the brass key on the lamp-room door? Petra does not know about the key.

Write the limerick Petra shouts up to Tomas as her boat comes alongside on a Thursday. One limerick: five lines, rhyming AABBA, anapestic, three beats in lines 1, 2, and 5 and two beats in lines 3 and 4. Name Tomas and mention the paraffin. Say nothing about the key. Return only the limerick.
