# Chapter 3: Night Flood

## Outline

1. **Return** - the water comes back all at once
2. **Smoothing** - the flood covers the day's prints
3. **Lamps** - the houses across the water, and the tides to come

---

## Chapter Text

It comes back the way a name comes back,
all at once, after you stop looking.

Over the mudflats, over the heron's prints,
over my own prints going out this morning,
the water lays its silver down
and makes the whole day smooth again.

I stand on the sea wall and count the lamps
across the water, one for each house
that will be there at the next low tide,
and the one after,
and the one after that.
