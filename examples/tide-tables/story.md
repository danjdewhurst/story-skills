# Tide Tables

## Synopsis

Three poems about one estuary across a day, from the dawn ebb to the night flood. The speaker walks out at low water, waits through slack water, and watches the flood come back at night.

## Tone & Style

Short free-verse lyrics in one speaker's present-tense voice. Plain words, long sentences broken across lines, and one image held at the end of each poem.
