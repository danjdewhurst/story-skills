# Gull Rock

## Description

A bare granite stack with a stone tower, a landing on the lee side, and a ledge on the east where the rock drops into the sea.

## History

The light has burned every night for forty years.

## Culture & Customs

Two keepers, one on watch and one asleep. The relief boat comes once a month.

## Notable Features

- The tower stair and the watch room below the lamp
- The oil store at the foot of the tower
- The storm bell below the gallery, rung by a rope outside the tower

## Current State

The lamp is dark when the story opens.
